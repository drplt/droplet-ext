## Name

Droplet Admin Extension

## Summary (≤132 characters)

Search text across your Droplet forms and datasets, and find any object by its ID, from a side panel.

## Description

The Droplet Admin Extension adds a side panel to the Droplet web app for builders and administrators.

**Find text** — Search every form or dataset in your organization for a word or phrase: field labels, workflow
assignees, notification text, translations, dataset values and more. The panel picks Forms or Datasets based on the
page you're on, and you can switch with one click. Results show exactly where each match is, with a highlighted
preview.

**Find by ID** — Paste any ID and instantly see whether it's a form, dataset, submission, eSign document, packet,
client, employee, role, tag or account.

Jump straight to any result on the current page, or open it in a new tab.

The Droplet Admin Extension works with your existing Droplet sign-in and respects your role's permissions. It does not collect
data, use analytics, or store your credentials.
